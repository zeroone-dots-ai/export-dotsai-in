# WhatsApp Outreach Sequences

> All sequences trigger from CRM stage changes. Tone: human, short, no marketing-speak.

---

## Sequence 1: Cold outbound (no prior contact)

### Day 0 — Intro

> Hi {first name} 👋
>
> Got your number from {source — make it specific: "your FIEO membership listing" / "your IndiaMart store for {product}"}.
>
> Quick reason for reaching out — we built a small platform that handles export documents, HS codes, and buyer discovery for Indian exporters. ₹3,000/month, 14 days free.
>
> Worth a 2-minute look? Here's the overview: {short link to landing}
>
> No pitch — happy to just answer questions if you have any.

### Day 2 — Soft follow-up (only if no reply)

> Just bumping this 👆
>
> If now's not the right time, no worries — what's a better month to revisit?

### Day 5 — Final touch

> Last note from me on this 🙏
>
> If exporting is going well and you don't need help, ignore. If you're curious, we have a free HS code calculator you can play with: {link}
>
> Either way, best of luck with {year/season} shipments.

## Tone rules (apply to all)

1. **Always use their first name.** Sounds basic, but most people don't.
2. **Reference specifics:** their product, their council, their city, their target market. Generic = ignored.
3. **No emojis if customer is 50+.** Use ✅ ✓ → instead of 🚀 🎉 etc.
4. **English + Hindi/Gujarati if you can.** "Aap ka next shipment kab hai?" beats "When is your next shipment?" for Indian SMEs.
5. **Voice notes > text** for high-trust moments (post-demo recap, lost-deal recovery). 20-30 second voice note from the founder = different category of impression.
6. **Never two messages in a row without a response.** Wait. Silence is a response.
