## Email rules

1. **Subject line < 50 characters.** No "🚀✨ Unlock your export potential 🌍" garbage.
2. **First sentence = the ask or the value.** No "Hope this finds you well."
3. **No images, no banners, no signature block with social icons.** Plain. Boring. Personal.
4. **Send from a real human address.** Tracking pixels OK; full HTML templates not OK.
5. **CC/BCC sparingly.** If you BCC your CRM for tracking, fine. Don't CC strangers.
6. **Unsubscribe link required by Indian DPDPA + CAN-SPAM.** At the bottom: "If you'd prefer no further emails, reply STOP."
