# Cold Call Script — export.dotsai.in

> One page. Practice once, internalize. Don't read on calls.

---

## Opening (0:00 — 0:20)

> "Hi, is this {first name}?
>
> {Pause for confirmation.}
>
> Hey {first name}, my name is {your name} from **ZeroOne Export** — we're a software platform for small Indian exporters. Quick 2-minute call — do you have a sec?
>
> {If no: 'No problem, when's a better time? I'll WhatsApp you the link in the meantime.'}
> {If yes: continue.}"

**Why this works:** You named yourself, your company, and the duration up front. No "is this a good time" weasel words.

---

## Qualify (0:20 — 2:00)

> "{first name}, I'm seeing you're listed as an exporter of {their product} on {source — Volza/FIEO/IndiaMart}. Is that still your main line?
>
> {Listen.}
>
> Cool. Quick question — how are you currently handling the documents side? Are you doing it yourself, or do you have a CA / consultant filing things?
>
> {Listen for: 'My CA does it', 'I use an agent', 'I do it myself in Excel', 'My uncle handles it'.}
>
> Got it. And what about HS codes and buyer discovery — are you finding buyers through {IndiaMart / Alibaba / referrals / trade fairs}?
>
> {Listen.}
>
> Got it."

**Goal:** confirm they're an exporter, understand their stack, find the gap.

---

## Pivot to value (2:00 — 4:00)

> "So here's what we do — we built a dashboard that handles four things in one place:
>
> 1. Document generation — Commercial Invoice, Packing List, BL instruction, Certificate of Origin. Type once, auto-fills.
> 2. HS code lookup with AI — describe your product in English, get the 8-digit code and the duty in your destination country.
> 3. Buyer discovery — we have shipment data showing who's actually importing your product in {their export country}, last 12 months.
> 4. License wizard — if you ever need to renew RCMC, file LUT, or onboard at a new port, our partner CA handles it.
>
> Pricing is ₹3,000 a month + 1% per shipment we help process, capped at ₹25,000 per shipment. So roughly the cost of one consultant call, for the whole thing.
>
> Most exporters we talk to are spending 4 — 6 hours a week on this stuff and paying a CA ₹10 — 15k a month. We bring that down."

---

## The ask (4:00 — 5:00)

> "Can I show you a 20-minute walkthrough this week? I'll fill in some real documents using your actual product, do a buyer search for {their target country}, and you can decide if it's worth ₹3k a month.
>
> What works — Thursday 4pm or Friday 11am?"

**Why two options:** "Yes or no" gets refused. "Pick A or B" is harder to dodge. If both bad, ask for their preference.

---

## Soft close + bookkeeping (5:00 — 6:00)

> "Perfect. I'll send the meet link on WhatsApp in 2 minutes. Two things before we hang up —
>
> 1. What's your WhatsApp number? Same as this one?
> 2. Briefly — what's the one thing about exporting you wish was easier? I'll make sure I cover that specifically in the demo."

**Why ask their pain:** lets you tailor the demo, demonstrates listening, gives you a hook for the WhatsApp followup.

## Common branches

**"Send me an email instead."**
→ "Sure — what's your email? And after I send it, can I check in on Friday to see if you've had a chance to look?" (Always get the next checkpoint.)

**"How are you different from Cogoport / Drip Capital?"**
→ "Cogoport does freight booking, Drip does trade finance. Both are good at what they do. We don't compete with them — we handle the parts that are *before* freight and *after* finance. Documents, compliance, HS, buyers. We work alongside them."

**"Are you a CHA?"**
→ "No — we're a software company. We work with CHA partners for actual customs clearance. We make sure your CHA gets clean documents and on-time instructions."

**"What if I don't ship that month?"**
→ "You just pay the ₹3,000 base. No commission if no shipment. And you can pause anytime — no contract."

**"I'll think about it." (the kiss of death)**
→ "Of course — what specifically would help you decide? Pricing? A specific feature? Trust?" → answer that → "OK, let me send you {specific thing}. I'll check in Friday."

## Calls per day target

40 dials → ~10 connects → 2 — 3 walkthroughs booked → 0.5 paid. Multiply by reps.

## Numbers to track in CRM after every call

- Dialed: yes/no
- Connected: yes/no
- Outcome: voicemail / wrong number / not interested / callback / demo booked
- Pain captured: free text
- Next action date: required if not "lost"
